Who We Are

About FSI

The Founders Support Initiative is launching its inaugural cohort to empower African entrepreneurs through grants, mentorship, and resources. Our mission is to foster innovation, support business growth, and create sustainable impact across communities. We're committed to a transparent, merit-based selection process for early-stage startups.

Funding

Grant Categories

Four distinct funding tracks for diverse entrepreneurial needs

General Startup Grant

For early-stage businesses across all sectors seeking seed funding to validate ideas and achieve product-market fit.

Youth Grant

Founders aged 18-35 building innovative solutions. Special focus on scalable business models with high growth potential.

Women in Business Grant

Supporting female-led enterprises with dedicated mentorship and funding to close the gender gap in entrepreneurship.

Innovation & Tech Grant

Tech solutions solving local problems in fintech, healthtech, edtech, agritech, and climate tech sectors.

Help

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers for applicants of our inaugural cohort

When will results be announced?

Our evaluation committee is currently reviewing all 298 eligible applications. Results will be announced in Q4 2026. All applicants will be notified via email, and the list will be published on this website. Please ensure you check the email you used for registration.

I paid but haven't received a confirmation email. What should I do?

If your payment was successful but you didn't get a confirmation, check your spam/junk folder first. If it's not there, email us at contact@founderssupport.org with your payment reference and full name. We'll verify and resend your confirmation within 48 hours.

Can I still apply? Is there a waitlist?

Applications for the 2026 inaugural cohort are officially closed. We do not accept late submissions. However, you can join our waitlist below to be notified when applications open for Cohort 2 in 2027.

How many grants will be awarded?

The exact number of grants depends on the quality of applications and available funding for this inaugural cycle. Our committee will shortlist based on innovation, impact potential, and business viability. Final numbers will be announced with the results.

What are the 4 grant categories?

1. General Startup Grant: Early-stage businesses across all sectors.
2. Youth Grant: Founders aged 18-35.
3. Women in Business Grant: Female-led enterprises.
4. Innovation & Tech Grant: Tech solutions in fintech, healthtech, edtech, agritech, climate tech.

What happens after I'm selected?

Selected entrepreneurs will be invited to a virtual onboarding session. You'll sign a grant agreement, receive the first disbursement, and be paired with a mentor. We'll also announce you publicly with your consent.

Is the ₦1,000 application fee refundable?

No, the application fee is non-refundable. It covers administrative costs for processing and reviewing applications. This was stated clearly during the application process.

Can I edit my application now?

No, applications are now locked for review. Edits were only allowed before payment submission. If you have critical updates, email contact@founderssupport.org, but we cannot guarantee changes will be considered at this stage.

How can I contact the FSI team?

For application-related queries: contact@founderssupport.org
For partnerships/media: contact@founderssupport.org
Follow us on Twitter/Instagram @founders support initiative.
