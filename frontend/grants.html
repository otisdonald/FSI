Why non-dilutive?

Most African founders give away 10-20% too early because it's the only capital available. We provide grants so you can hit meaningful milestones before raising priced equity. Keep ownership, prove traction, raise on your terms.

Eligibility

  • Based in Africa or African diaspora building for Africa
  • Age 18-45 (Youth Grant: 18-35)
  • Early-stage or growth with MVP/traction
  • All sectors welcome

We especially encourage:

Female founders, teams outside Lagos/Nairobi, and startups solving real infrastructure problems.

Frequently asked

Do I repay the grant?

No. It's a grant, not a loan. No equity, no repayment.

What if I'm not Nigerian?

We pay in local currency equivalent via Flutterwave. Available across Africa.

Can I apply to multiple categories?

No, choose one. We'll move you if you're a better fit elsewhere.

Is the ₦1,000 fee refundable?

No, it covers processing. It prevents spam applications.
