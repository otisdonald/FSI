About FSI

Closing the gap between ideas and execution

The Founders Support Initiative exists to turn ambitious ideas into impactful, scalable businesses across Africa. Too many promising ventures never leave the drawing board due to lack of capital, guidance, or the right network. FSI was created to close that gap.

Our Mission

To unlock the next generation of African innovators by bridging the gap between ideas and execution. We back ventures that are profitable, scalable, and sustainable — businesses that grow across borders and create meaningful jobs.

The FSI Promise

We don't just fund. We build alongside you. We don't just teach. We connect you to people who've done it before. We don't just talk impact. We back ventures that solve real problems for real people.

Everything you need to build, in one place
