Opportunities FAQ

Do I need to pay for competitions or jobs?

Never. Only the initial grant application has a ₦1,000 fee to prevent spam. Competitions and job access are free for all applicants.

I'm not in Nigeria, can I still apply?

Yes. We fund founders across Africa. Grants are paid in local currency equivalent via Flutterwave.

Competitions in Q3 2026, Jobs board in Q4. Join the waitlist on each card and we'll email you first.
