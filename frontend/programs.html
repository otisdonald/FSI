The 12-Week Journey

We don't dump everything on day one. Each phase builds on the last.

WEEKS 1-3

Foundation

Problem validation, customer interviews, refine MVP. First grant tranche released.

WEEKS 4-6

Build

Product sprint, pricing tests, first 10 customers. Mentor deep dives.

WEEKS 7-9

Grow

GTM playbook, hiring first roles, metrics dashboard. Second tranche.

WEEKS 10-12

Raise

Pitch prep, investor intros, demo day. Graduate to alumni network.

Common questions

Do I have to give up equity?

No. Grants are non-dilutive. Mentorship and training are included. We may ask for a small option to invest in your next round at market terms, but it's optional.

Can I apply if I'm solo?

Yes, but we strongly prefer teams of 2-3. Solo founders have a harder time. We'll help you find a co-founder during the program if needed.

What if I don't get the grant?

You still get access to training and community. About 40% of applicants get funding, 100% get the program if accepted.
